The company hires only females who are trained in providing pre and post delivery services. Personnel must have updated CPR training and be able to work irregular hours. The company provides clients with Doula services at a flat rate that covers remote(telephone consultation) only. 

Home and hospital visits are additional and are charged an hourly rate separate from the base/flat rate. 

You have agreed to develop a system that tracks the work of the doula and provides the clients with a bill that includes:
- the patient's information (the pregnant mother), 
- the base cost, 
- a list of the additional services rendered along with costs and 
- a total amount for the billing period.
