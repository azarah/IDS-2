Investment thesis (summary)

Doula-led care blends remote continuity (telephone reassurance, scheduling, documentation touchpoints) with episodic, high-touch visits that accrue hourly, mileage, and overnight complexity.

Small practices routinely lose margin and trust when billing is improvised. A disciplined, policy-encoded billing surface becomes infrastructure as payers—including Illinois Medicaid frameworks and regulated commercial coverage—professionalize reimbursement.

We are positioning a standards-forward, file-backed invoicing brain that travels from today’s workstation trust boundary to governed hosting and payer integrations—with minimal dependency surface.

Market contextLandscape · 03

Pain IDS encodes explicitly

  • Mixed monetization semantics: flat-rate telephone bundles versus hourly hospital/home labor, mileage, overnight holds—each mapped to discrete catalog codes (SERVICE_CATALOG) and rate kinds.
  • Operator ambiguity: without guided menus (prompt_service_code_choice), line items degrade into handwritten descriptions that reconcile poorly.
  • Roster integrity: client ↔ doula assignment drift creates disputes; admin visibility into orphan IDs is prerequisite for scale.
  • Arithmetic credibility: currency must Quantize cleanly at cents boundaries (Decimal, ROUND_HALF_UP).

Invoice anatomy (dual subtotal)

Families deserve transparent separation:

  • Base (telephone / flat) assignments aggregate under policy tele rates.
  • Additional services summarize hospital/home hourly, mileage, overnight, etc.
  • Total Due closes the labelled billing period mirrored from rate configuration.

Moat hypotheses & wedge extensions

  • Policy-as-data — rate catalogs + enumerated service codes outperform generic spreadsheets when audits arrive.
  • Composable migration path — plaintext SSOT eases ingestion from CSV/Sheets tooling before governed databases.
  • Compliance runway — intentionally scoped baseline; HIPAA, MFA, payer 837 payloads require funded hardening—not science projects.
  • Empathic tone — prompts remain assistive (“reduce doula-client-admin friction”), not adversarial—a brand moat rarely copied by incumbent billing blobs.
