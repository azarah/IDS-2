## Sources — Illinois reimbursement & billing context

This sample is a **client-facing statement / worksheet** helpful for IDS records and payer coordination. Actual **Illinois Medicaid** reimbursement is billed via **837 Professional (837P)** or **MEDI** per HFS; **commercial payers** use each insurer’s guides. IDS should verify codes, modifiers, POS, units, and documentation with a qualified biller or payer liaison.

### Illinois Medicaid (HFS) — primary

- [Illinois HFS — Doula fee schedule & downloads](https://hfs.illinois.gov/medicalproviders/medicaidreimbursement/doula.html)
- [HFS provider notice — Doula certification, IMPACT enrollment, billing (Public Act 102-0004; effective Feb 1, 2024)](https://hfs.illinois.gov/medicalproviders/notices/notice.prn241219b.html)
- [Illinois General Assembly — HB 0354 / Public Act 102-0004 full text](https://ilga.gov/legislation/fulltext.asp?DocName=10200HB0354&GA=102&SessionId=110&DocTypeId=HB&LegID=128258&DocNum=354&GAID=16&SpecSess=&Session=&print=true)
- [SIU School of Medicine — Illinois Medicaid-Certified Doula Program](https://www.siumed.edu/medicine/imcdp) (certification prerequisite for Medicaid enrollment pathway per HFS)
- [CMS — NPPES NPI Registry](https://npiregistry.cms.hhs.gov/) · [NUCC provider taxonomy — e.g. 374J00000X (doula)](https://www.nucc.org/index.php/code-sets-mainmenu-41/provider-taxonomy-mainmenu-40/current-taxonomy-display)

Locate the current **Chapter 300 Companion Guide** for **837P** from [Illinois HFS medical providers](https://hfs.illinois.gov/medicalproviders/medicalcoverage.html) (linked from notices; URL may update with revisions).
